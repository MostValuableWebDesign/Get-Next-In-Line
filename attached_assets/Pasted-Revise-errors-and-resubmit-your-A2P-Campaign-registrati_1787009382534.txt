Campaign description
This campaign sends customer care and appointment-related SMS messages from the GetNextInLine app to customers who have opted in during the barber shop check-in process. Messages include queue confirmation, estimated wait times, queue position updates, notifications when the customer is next in line, alerts when the barber is ready, appointment status updates, and responses to customer support inquiries. These messages are intended to provide real-time queue management and improve the customer check-in experience. Customers may reply to receive assistance or manage their queue status.

How do end-users consent to receive messages?
Customers opt in by providing their mobile phone number during the digital check-in process at a participating barber shop. Before joining the queue, customers are presented with a checkbox that states they agree to receive SMS notifications related to their visit, including queue confirmations, estimated wait times, queue position updates, and notifications when it is their turn or when their barber is ready. Customers must actively check the consent box before submitting the form. Links to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy are displayed during the check-in process. Customers may reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for assistance.
