GNIL is a single-client, single-business application.

Product rule:

```text
One deployment
=
One client
=
One business
```

The core `/sos/*` and `/operations/*` runtime already follows this model.

However, the repository still contains platform-style multi-business product surfaces such as:

* Tenant Operations
* Tenant Detail
* Governance business assignment
* franchise/multi-location administration
* multi-business billing/reporting
* cross-business administration
* business provisioning/simulation
* platform business targeting

For this product, these should NOT exist as normal application features.

DO NOT change the internal tenant_id database scoping strategy.
DO NOT remove tenant foreign keys broadly.
DO NOT change public booking slug behavior.
DO NOT break signed webhooks.
DO NOT remove co-op counterparty IDs where they represent another business in a B2B transaction.

## 1. Audit multi-business UI/routes

Search for:

```text
Tenant Operations
Tenant Detail
tenants
tenant management
business assignments
franchise
multi-location
organization switcher
business provisioning
platform billing
tenant reporting
business targeting
workspace
installation
```

Classify each occurrence as:

```text
normal client feature
platform/admin feature
internal infrastructure
cross-business transaction identity
```

## 2. Remove normal access to platform-style multi-business pages

Remove from the normal GNIL client navigation:

* Tenant Operations
* Tenant Detail
* multi-business dashboards
* tenant/business provisioning
* franchise controller
* cross-installation reports
* platform billing controls
* business assignment management
* business-targeted platform broadcasts

The business owner should never encounter these.

## 3. Route handling

For routes that exist only to administer multiple GNIL client businesses, either:

* remove them if truly unused, or
* isolate them behind a clearly separate internal platform/admin surface

Do not leave them casually mounted as normal merchant application features.

If retaining any internal route, document exactly why.

## 4. Governance cleanup

Normal business users should manage:

```text
their staff
their roles
their permissions
```

They should not assign users across businesses or tenants.

Remove any normal UI for cross-business membership assignment.

Keep ordinary role-based authorization.

## 5. Franchise/multi-location

This GNIL deployment is one business.

Do not retain a multi-location franchise controller as a normal feature.

Do not add a location selector.

If basic address/location data exists, treat it as attributes of the single business.

## 6. Platform billing/reporting

Normal owners should only see billing/reporting relevant to their own GNIL deployment.

Remove:

```text
all-tenants revenue
cross-business module reporting
multi-tenant billing summaries
installation comparison
```

from the client experience.

## 7. Preserve legitimate cross-business domain concepts

Do NOT confuse multi-business administration with B2B/co-op relationships.

A co-op transaction may legitimately reference:

```text
host business
counterparty business
partner business
```

Those IDs may remain because they represent transaction participants, not a user-selected app context.

Document these explicitly.

## 8. Frontend navigation

Search all navigation, command center, sidebar, settings, marketplace, admin menus, and route registries.

The normal client app should conceptually contain:


not:

```text

```

## 9. Tests

Add regression tests proving normal merchant/front-desk/staff users cannot navigate to multi-business administration.

Remove obsolete frontend tests that protect tenant-switching/platform-business UX.

Do not remove backend isolation tests.

Search repository again for:

Return:

* pages/routes removed

* tests

