Build a complete, production-ready full-stack multi-tenant web application, booking, and local growth platform called "Get Next In Line". The platform must support any local storefront business or service provider (such as barbershops, salons, schools, auto repair, wellness studios, or retail service shops). ### Tech Stack Specifications - Frontend: React or Next.js with Tailwind CSS for a modern, mobile-responsive, clean UI. - Backend: Node.js / Express (or Next.js API routes). - Database: PostgreSQL (via Prisma ORM or Drizzle) to handle secure multi-tenant data isolation. ### Core Modules & Features to Implement 1. Multi-Tenant Architecture & Business Profiles: - Allow different business owners to sign up, configure their profile, select their industry type, and manage their own unique booking page URL slug (e.g., app.com/business-name). - Support flexible operational staff management settings (including commission tracking models versus traditional flat fee or booth rental models). 2. Simplified, All-Inclusive Service Menu System: - Provide a clean, all-inclusive service menu structure focusing on flat-rate packages and treatments rather than cluttered a la carte add-ons. - Allow businesses to customize service categories, descriptions, transparent pricing, and estimated durations. 3. Direct Booking & Scheduling Engine: - A frictionless client-facing scheduling interface where customers can pick a service, select a staff member, choose an available time slot, and book directly without third-party marketplace interference. - Calendar management view for staff and owners to track daily, weekly, and monthly appointments. - Flexible integration options, including a standalone landing page or an embeddable widget/subdomain that business owners can easily connect to their existing website (such as WordPress, Shopify, Wix, or Squarespace). 4. Customer Relationship Management (CRM) & Retention: - Client directory that securely logs customer contact info, service history, visit frequency, and total lifetime spend. - Automated retention triggers / reminder logic placeholder to track customer visit frequency and prompt re-engagement. 5. Multi-Engine Local SEO & Technical Foundation: - Dynamic landing page and subdomain generation for each storefront featuring localized metadata and structured Schema.org local business markup to ensure search engine crawlers can properly index business hours, address, phone numbers, and services. - Built-in review and customer engagement display sections to capture high-intent local search traffic and support optimal local map pack visibility.
